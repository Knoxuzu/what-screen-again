# What Screen Again? An Interactive Story-Based Website

"What Screen Again?" is an interactive story-based website that explores how presenting an online persona can influence the way individuals perceive and understand their identity offline. Through a fictional storyline, users navigate different situations, make decisions, and experience how their choices influence the progression and outcomes of the story. The project focuses on how factors such as peer influence, social comparison, online attention, and the pursuit of approval can shape an individual's perception of themselves and their personal identity.

Disclaimer: "What Screen Again?" is an academic project developed for educational and awareness purposes. Its storyline, characters, and scenarios are fictional and are intended to encourage reflection on how online self-presentation may influence personal identity. The website does not provide psychological advice, diagnose mental health conditions, or claim to represent everyone's experiences with social media and online identity.

Project Status: Currently in development.
